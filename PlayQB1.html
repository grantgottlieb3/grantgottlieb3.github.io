How PlayQB1 works

You are the quarterback

You play every offensive snap in first person. Call a play, read the defense before the snap, then aim with the mouse, hold to set throw power, and let it go. Escape the rush with WASD, or tuck it and run.

Your defense plays on the sideline

When the other team has the ball, you watch from the sideline. Sim their drive on the tablet one play at a time or all at once, check Film, then take the field.

A real defense

Defenses play Cover 0, 1, 2, 3, and 4, man and zone, plus fire zones and blitzes. They disguise one look before the snap and rotate into another, linemen win their rushes and bat balls, and a spy can shadow you. Motion tells you man or zone: a defender who follows the motion man is in man.

Game plans and film

Before kickoff you set your defense’s game plan, and the other side has one too. Film on the tablet shows what coverages they have played and how your plays are doing, and after a drive you can adjust your plan.

    How multiplayer works

    On this computer

    Two players share one computer and take turns. The host picks the game settings (quarter length, home team, defense skill, chew clock, and crowd shake), then each of you picks a team name, colors, and a defensive game plan in private, and you trade drives. When your drive ends, pass the computer. Your friend lands on the sideline with a summary of your drive on the tablet, checks Film if they like, and takes the field.

    Online with a friend Coming soon

    Same game, two computers. Create a game and share the code, or join with your friend’s code. After your drive, stay on the sideline to watch for their drive in real time, or leave and come back later.

    Defensive game plan

    You never call defense play by play. Before kickoff you set how your defense plays all game: man or zone, how often it blitzes and disguises, what it does on 3rd and long, in the red zone, and at the goal line, and one play type to sell out against. Your friend’s offense faces that plan on every snap, and it can’t be changed during the game.

    Film

    On the sideline, tap Film on the tablet to see how your plays are doing and what coverages their defense has shown you so far.

    Leave this game?

    Leave this game? It’s saved, so you can pick it back up from the main menu.
